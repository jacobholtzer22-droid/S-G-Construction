## Four kinds of work

Everything S&G Construction does falls into one of four categories. ADU construction means adding a second, self-contained home to a lot that already has a house on it, whether that is a detached new build or a conversion of space you already have. Full home remodels take most or all of the rooms in a house as one project instead of one at a time. Kitchen and bathroom remodels are the two rooms people most often want reworked on their own, and both can run from new finishes in the existing layout to moving the layout itself.

What they have in common is that they are whole projects. S&G Construction is not a handyman service and does not take on small repairs or basic installations.
